Questions, answered

Before you download

Do I need a subscription?

No. FreelanceOS is a one-time digital purchase. There is no recurring subscription for the workbook.

Do I need an internet connection?

No internet connection is required for normal use after you download the files.

Which spreadsheet app should I use?

The workbook is designed for Microsoft Excel 2016 or later. It has been tested in LibreOffice Calc 24.2. Excel and Google Sheets have not yet been independently verified.

Does it handle GST filing or e-invoicing?

No. It is an operational record-keeping tool, not a GST filing, e-invoicing or accounting compliance product. Check tax requirements with a qualified professional.

Can I share or resell the workbook?

The included licence is for a single user. Redistribution and resale are not permitted.
